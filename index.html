About

Designing with focus,
learning without limits.

I'm Omar Osama, a media & graphic designer and student based in Cairo, Egypt. My work sits at the intersection of brand identity, social media design, photography and interface design — I'm drawn to details that most people scroll past.

Every project starts the same way: a single aperture, wide open, taking in as much of the brief as possible before narrowing to the one idea worth making. That's the instinct behind the mark I design under — and behind how I approach a page, a feed, or a product screen.

Outside client work, I lead design on academic group projects and keep building fluency in the tools — from Adobe Creative Cloud to front-end code — that let an idea move from sketch to shipped.
